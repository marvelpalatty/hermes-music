Personal-use integration

Hermes Music

A personal connection between the account owner’s Hermes assistant and YouTube, maintained by marvelpalatty.

What it does

  • Reads the authorized account’s YouTube channel identity, owned playlists, playlist items and liked videos.
  • Searches the public YouTube Music catalog without using a personal browser session.
  • Supports an owner-controlled workflow: any future enabled playlist or rating edits require the owner’s specific command or approval.

What it does not do

This is not a complete YouTube Music integration. Music-specific saved songs, albums and artists, listening history, and personalized recommendations are not available through the deployed OAuth connection. YouTube liked videos may include non-music content.

Account authorization

Google authorization is completed on Google’s own pages. OAuth credentials and tokens are held on the owner’s private server, not on this website or in its public repository. The current deployed tool surface is read-only.

This page is informational: it does not accept passwords, tokens or music-library uploads.
